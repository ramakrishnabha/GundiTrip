Oxford & The Cotswolds
Wed 28 – Fri 30 May · The Royal Leonardo Hotel
28 – 30 May · 3 Days
The Dreaming Spires & Storybook Villages ✨
Weekend Away
Day 1 — Oxford Magic
Day 2 — Cotswolds Villages
Day 3 — Palaces & Punts
🌦️13–20°CLight drizzle — pack a light jacket, still lovely for exploring28 May last yr
🧙‍♂️
Christ Church College — The Real Hogwarts
The dining hall inspired the Great Hall. The grand staircase appeared in the films. Absolute must-see for any HP fan!
📚
Bodleian Library & Radcliffe Camera
Hogwarts library scenes were filmed here. The round Radcliffe Camera is jaw-dropping — one of the most photographed buildings in the world
🗼
Carfax Tower — Climb for the View
99 steps up for a panoramic view over Oxford's dreaming spires. Only £3!
🏪
Oxford Covered Market
A hidden gem since 1774 — amazing food stalls, independent boutiques, jewellery, candles, clothing. Perfect for gifts and treats!
📖
Blackwell's Bookshop
The most famous bookshop in Oxford — 3 miles of shelves underground. Perfect for picking up a new HP edition or a travel memento!
🍺
The Eagle & Child Pub
Where Tolkien and C.S. Lewis used to share their writing. Order a Coke and soak it all in — the origin of Middle-earth and Narnia!
💡Wear comfy shoes — Oxford is best explored on foot. Most colleges charge a small entry fee but are totally worth it.
🌦️13–24°CWarm with light drizzle — Cotswolds looks magical in the mist!29 May last yr
🦆
Bourton-on-the-Water
"The Venice of the Cotswolds" — low stone bridges over a clear river, ducks everywhere. Grab an ice cream and paddle your feet!
🏘️
Bibury — Arlington Row
The most photographed row of cottages in England — honey-coloured stone, flower boxes, absolute fairytale. This is literally on the UK passport!
🌸
Burford — The Gateway to the Cotswolds
A gorgeous sloping high street lined with antique shops, boutiques and gift stores. Perfect afternoon tea + serious browsing potential!
🔭
Broadway Tower
A folly on the second highest point in the Cotswolds — on a clear day you can see 13 counties! Stunning sunset spot
🫖
Afternoon Tea in a Cotswolds Tearoom
Scones with clotted cream and jam is non-negotiable Cotswolds tradition. Find the cosiest tearoom you can!
🚗The Cotswolds is best by car — villages are spread out but each one is more magical than the last. Give yourself time to wander!
🌦️14–25°CWarm and mostly dry — great day for Blenheim's grounds30 May last yr
👑
Blenheim Palace
UNESCO World Heritage Site — birthplace of Winston Churchill. The grounds alone are worth it: lakes, Italian gardens, butterfly house. Absolutely stunning!
🛶
Punting on the Cherwell
The most Oxford thing you can do! Glide past the colleges on a punt. Go guided or DIY — falling in is a rite of passage 😄
🎨
Ashmolean Museum
Britain's oldest public museum — free entry! Amazing art from ancient Egypt to the Renaissance
🌺
Oxford Botanic Garden
The oldest botanic garden in the UK. Philip Pullman fans: this is where Lyra and Will meet in His Dark Materials — same magical universe as HP!
🌅For sunset, head to South Park — free hilltop with the most spectacular view of Oxford's skyline you'll ever see.

Big Day!

  • 🏰
    Tower Bridge
    Walk across the glass floor walkway for incredible views — don't look down! 😅

    Leadenhall Market — Diagon Alley!
    This stunning Victorian covered market was used as the entrance to Diagon Alley in the HP films. The Optician's was the Leaky Cauldron!
  • ⛪
    St Paul's Cathedral
    Climb to the Whispering Gallery — whisper to the wall and it carries your voice all the way around!
  • 🕰️
    Big Ben & Westminster
    The classic London shot — grab it at golden hour if you can!
  • 🎡
    London Eye
    Book in advance — the views at sunset are unreal on a clear day like this one!
  • 🌉
    Millennium Bridge
    Walk the bridge that Death Eaters destroy in Harry Potter and the Half-Blood Prince! Then explore the free Tate Modern gallery next door
  • 🛍️
    Oxford Circus — Shopping Heaven
    Carnaby Street, Regent Street, Oxford Street — the shopping heart of London. ASOS flagship, Zara, Topshop, Liberty London, everything!
🚇Get an Oyster card or use contactless — cheapest way around London. The Elizabeth line is new, fast and beautiful.
