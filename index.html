Oxford & The Cotswolds
Wed 28 May – Fri 30 May · The Royal Leonardo Hotel

The Dreaming Spires & Storybook Villages ✨
Weekend Away
Day 1 — Oxford Magic
Day 2 — Cotswolds Villages
Day 3 — Palaces & Punts
🧙‍♂️
Christ Church College — The Real Hogwarts
The dining hall inspired the Great Hall in Harry Potter. The grand staircase appeared in the films. Absolute must!
📚
Bodleian Library & Radcliffe Camera
One of the oldest libraries in the world — Hogwarts library scenes were filmed here. The round Radcliffe Camera is jaw-dropping
🗼
Carfax Tower — Climb for the View
Climb 99 steps for a panoramic view over all of Oxford's dreaming spires. Only £3!
🏪
Oxford Covered Market
A hidden gem since 1774 — amazing food stalls, independent boutiques, and the best hot chocolate in Oxford
📖
Blackwell's Bookshop
The most famous bookshop in Oxford — the underground Norrington Room holds 3 miles of shelves. Mind-blowing!
🍺
The Eagle & Child Pub
Where J.R.R. Tolkien and C.S. Lewis used to meet and share their writing. Order a Coke and soak it all in!
💡Wear comfy shoes — Oxford is best explored on foot. Most colleges charge a small entry fee but are totally worth it.
🦆
Bourton-on-the-Water
"The Venice of the Cotswolds" — low stone bridges over a clear river, ducks everywhere. Grab an ice cream and paddle your feet!
🏘️
Bibury — Arlington Row
The most photographed row of cottages in England. Honey-coloured stone, flower boxes, absolute fairytale vibes. This is on the UK passport!
🌸
Burford — The Gateway to the Cotswolds
A gorgeous sloping high street lined with antique shops, tea rooms and boutiques. Perfect for a wander and afternoon tea
🔭
Broadway Tower
A folly on the second highest point in the Cotswolds — on a clear day you can see 13 counties! Stunning sunset spot
🫖
Afternoon Tea in a Cotswolds Tearoom
Find a cosy tearoom in any village — scones with clotted cream and jam is non-negotiable Cotswolds tradition
🚗The Cotswolds is best by car — villages are spread out but each one is more magical than the last. Give yourself time to wander and get happily lost.
👑
Blenheim Palace
A UNESCO World Heritage Site just 8 miles from Oxford — birthplace of Winston Churchill. The grounds alone are worth the visit: lakes, gardens, butterfly house. Absolutely stunning!
🛶
Punting on the Cherwell
The most Oxford thing you can do! Hire a punt and glide down the river past the colleges. Go with a guided boat or DIY — falling in is a rite of passage 😄
🎨
Ashmolean Museum
Britain's oldest public museum — free entry! Amazing art and antiquities from ancient Egypt to the Renaissance
🌺
Oxford Botanic Garden
The oldest botanic garden in the UK — beautiful walled gardens along the river. Philip Pullman fans: this is where Lyra and Will meet in His Dark Materials!
🌅For sunset, head to South Park — free hilltop park with the most spectacular view of Oxford's skyline you'll ever see.

Dinosaurs, History & Pigeons 🦕
Free Museums!
  • 🦕
    Natural History Museum
    Free entry! The blue whale skeleton, the dinosaur hall, the giant sequoia — genuinely mind-blowing. Book a free timed entry online first
  • ⚔️
    Imperial War Museum
    Incredibly moving exhibitions on WWI, WWII, and modern conflicts. The Holocaust gallery is sobering and important
  • 🐦
    Trafalgar Square
    Feed the pigeons (if you dare!), see the Fourth Plinth art installation, and soak up the energy of one of the world's great public squares

Windsor & Buckingham Palace 👑
Royal Day
  • 🚂
    Train from London Victoria
    ~40 min train ride — a lovely journey through the English countryside
  • 🏰
    Windsor Castle
    The oldest and largest occupied castle in the world — home to the Royal Family. St George's Chapel is breathtaking. Try to catch the Changing of the Guard!
  • 🌳
    Windsor Great Park
    3,800 acres of parkland right next to the castle — beautiful Long Walk leading up to the castle is iconic
  • 🎺
    Buckingham Palace
    Back in London — check the schedule for the Changing of the Guard ceremony at the Palace
⏰Book Windsor Castle tickets in advance online — queues can be very long, especially in summer.
